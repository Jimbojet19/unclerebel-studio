Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

UncleRebel Studio is used only by its owner, on his own accounts.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission: the owner's basic profile (name, avatar, username), account stats, his own videos, and comments and messages on his own posts, through the official TikTok, Instagram and YouTube APIs.

Where it is kept

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer (Windows Credential Manager or a local file). Nothing is sold, shared or sent to third parties, and no data about other users is collected or stored beyond what is needed to show and reply to comments on the owner's posts.

Removing access

The owner can disconnect the tool at any time in the TikTok, Instagram or Google account settings, which revokes its access.

Questions: message @uncle.rebel on Instagram.
